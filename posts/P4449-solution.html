题目链接

P4449

题意

给定 kkk，询问 TTT 次，每次询问给出 n,mn,mn,m，求

∑i=1n∑j=1mgcd⁡(i,j)k\displaystyle \sum_{i=1}^n\sum_{j=1}^m\gcd(i,j)^k i=1∑n​j=1∑m​gcd(i,j)k

答案对 109+710^9+7109+7 取模。

解

大力推式子（认为 n≤mn \leq mn≤m）：

    ∑i=1n∑j=1mgcd⁡(i,j)k\displaystyle \ \ \ \ \sum_{i=1}^n\sum_{j=1}^m\gcd(i,j)^k    i=1∑n​j=1∑m​gcd(i,j)k

=∑d=1ndk∑i=1n∑j=1m[gcd⁡(i,j)=d]\displaystyle = \sum_{d=1}^nd^k\sum_{i=1}^n\sum_{j=1}^m[\gcd(i,j)=d]=d=1∑n​dki=1∑n​j=1∑m​[gcd(i,j)=d]

=∑d=1ndk∑i=1⌊nd⌋∑j=1⌊md⌋[i⊥j]\displaystyle = \sum_{d=1}^nd^k\sum_{i=1}^{\left\lfloor\frac{n}{d}\right\rfloor}\sum_{j=1}^{\left\lfloor\frac{m}{d}\right\rfloor}[i \perp j]=d=1∑n​dki=1∑⌊dn​⌋​j=1∑⌊dm​⌋​[i⊥j]

=∑d=1ndk∑i=1⌊nd⌋∑j=1⌊md⌋∑q∣gcd⁡(i,j)μ(q)\displaystyle = \sum_{d=1}^nd^k\sum_{i=1}^{\left\lfloor\frac{n}{d}\right\rfloor}\sum_{j=1}^{\left\lfloor\frac{m}{d}\right\rfloor}\sum_{q|\gcd(i,j)}\mu(q)=d=1∑n​dki=1∑⌊dn​⌋​j=1∑⌊dm​⌋​q∣gcd(i,j)∑​μ(q)

=∑d=1ndk∑q=1⌊nd⌋μ(q)∑i=1⌊nd⌋[i∣q]∑j=1⌊md⌋[j∣q]\displaystyle = \sum_{d=1}^nd^k\sum_{q=1}^{\left\lfloor\frac{n}{d}\right\rfloor}\mu(q)\sum_{i=1}^{\left\lfloor\frac{n}{d}\right\rfloor}[i|q]\sum_{j=1}^{\left\lfloor\frac{m}{d}\right\rfloor}[j|q]=d=1∑n​dkq=1∑⌊dn​⌋​μ(q)i=1∑⌊dn​⌋​[i∣q]j=1∑⌊dm​⌋​[j∣q]

=∑d=1ndk∑q=1⌊nd⌋μ(q)⌊ndq⌋⌊mdq⌋\displaystyle = \sum_{d=1}^nd^k\sum_{q=1}^{\left\lfloor\frac{n}{d}\right\rfloor}\mu(q)\left\lfloor\frac{n}{dq}\right\rfloor\left\lfloor\frac{m}{dq}\right\rfloor=d=1∑n​dkq=1∑⌊dn​⌋​μ(q)⌊dqn​⌋⌊dqm​⌋

=∑t=1n∑d∣tdkμ(td)⌊nt⌋⌊mt⌋\displaystyle = \sum_{t=1}^n\sum_{d|t}d^k\mu\left(\frac{t}{d}\right)\left\lfloor\frac{n}{t}\right\rfloor\left\lfloor\frac{m}{t}\right\rfloor=t=1∑n​d∣t∑​dkμ(dt​)⌊tn​⌋⌊tm​⌋

=∑t=1n⌊nt⌋⌊mt⌋∑d∣tdkμ(td)\displaystyle = \sum_{t=1}^n\left\lfloor\frac{n}{t}\right\rfloor\left\lfloor\frac{m}{t}\right\rfloor\sum_{d|t}d^k\mu\left(\frac{t}{d}\right)=t=1∑n​⌊tn​⌋⌊tm​⌋d∣t∑​dkμ(dt​)

记 fk(n)=nkf_k(n) = n^kfk​(n)=nk，则原式

=∑t=1n⌊nt⌋⌊mt⌋(μ∗fk)(t)\displaystyle = \sum_{t=1}^n\left\lfloor\frac{n}{t}\right\rfloor\left\lfloor\frac{m}{t}\right\rfloor(\mu \ast f_k)(t)=t=1∑n​⌊tn​⌋⌊tm​⌋(μ∗fk​)(t)

考虑如何筛出 gk=μ∗fkg_k = \mu \ast f_kgk​=μ∗fk​：

对于质数 ppp，

{gk(1)=1gk(p)=pk−1gk(pα)=p(α−1)k(pk−1)\begin{cases} g_k(1) = 1 \\ g_k( p ) = p^k - 1 \\ g_k(p^\alpha) = p^{(\alpha-1)k}(p^k - 1) \end{cases}⎩⎨⎧​gk​(1)=1gk​(p)=pk−1gk​(pα)=p(α−1)k(pk−1)​

从而线性筛解决。

质数的 kkk 次幂，在线性筛时求出即可。

时间复杂度：O(Nlog⁡k+Tn)\displaystyle \mathrm{O} \left(N\log k + T\sqrt{n}\right)O(Nlogk+Tn​)

解题笔记—搬运

首先，有结论

d(a,b)=∑i∣a∑j∣b[i⊥j]\displaystyle d(a,b) = \sum_{i|a}\sum_{j|b}[i \perp j] d(a,b)=i∣a∑​j∣b∑​[i⊥j]

故原式可化为

∑i=1N∑j=1M∑x∣i∑y∣j[x⊥y]\displaystyle \sum_{i=1}^N\sum_{j=1}^M\sum_{x|i}\sum_{y|j}[x \perp y] i=1∑N​j=1∑M​x∣i∑​y∣j∑​[x⊥y]

下面作变换：

=∑i=1N∑j=1M[i⊥j]⌊Ni⌋⌊Mj⌋\displaystyle = \sum_{i=1}^N\sum_{j=1}^M[i \perp j]\left\lfloor\frac{N}{i}\right\rfloor\left\lfloor\frac{M}{j}\right\rfloor=i=1∑N​j=1∑M​[i⊥j]⌊iN​⌋⌊jM​⌋

=∑i=1N∑j=1Mε(gcd⁡(i,j))⌊Ni⌋⌊Mj⌋\displaystyle = \sum_{i=1}^N\sum_{j=1}^M\varepsilon(\gcd(i,j))\left\lfloor\frac{N}{i}\right\rfloor\left\lfloor\frac{M}{j}\right\rfloor=i=1∑N​j=1∑M​ε(gcd(i,j))⌊iN​⌋⌊jM​⌋

=∑d=1min⁡(N,M)μ(d)∑i=1⌊Nd⌋∑j=1⌊Md⌋⌊Ndi⌋⌊Mdj⌋\displaystyle = \sum_{d=1}^{\min(N,M)}\mu(d)\sum_{i=1}^{\left\lfloor\frac{N}{d}\right\rfloor}\sum_{j=1}^{\left\lfloor\frac{M}{d}\right\rfloor}\left\lfloor\frac{N}{di}\right\rfloor\left\lfloor\frac{M}{dj}\right\rfloor=d=1∑min(N,M)​μ(d)i=1∑⌊dN​⌋​j=1∑⌊dM​⌋​⌊diN​⌋⌊djM​⌋

接下来，反用结论得到

∑d=1min⁡(N,M)μ(d)∑i=1⌊Nd⌋∑j=1⌊Md⌋⌊⌊Nd⌋i⌋⌊⌊Md⌋j⌋\displaystyle \sum_{d=1}^{\min(N,M)}\mu(d)\sum_{i=1}^{\left\lfloor\frac{N}{d}\right\rfloor}\sum_{j=1}^{\left\lfloor\frac{M}{d}\right\rfloor}\left\lfloor\frac{\left\lfloor\frac{N}{d}\right\rfloor}{i}\right\rfloor\left\lfloor\frac{\left\lfloor\frac{M}{d}\right\rfloor}{j}\right\rfloor d=1∑min(N,M)​μ(d)i=1∑⌊dN​⌋​j=1∑⌊dM​⌋​⌊i⌊dN​⌋​⌋⌊j⌊dM​⌋​⌋

不妨记

f(n)=∑i=1n⌊ni⌋\displaystyle f(n) = \sum_{i=1}^n\left\lfloor\frac{n}{i}\right\rfloor f(n)=i=1∑n​⌊in​⌋

则答案最终为

∑d=1min⁡(N,M)μ(d)f(⌊Nd⌋)f(⌊Md⌋)\displaystyle \sum_{d=1}^{\min(N,M)}\mu(d)f\left(\left\lfloor\frac{N}{d}\right\rfloor\right)f\left(\left\lfloor\frac{M}{d}\right\rfloor\right) d=1∑min(N,M)​μ(d)f(⌊dN​⌋)f(⌊dM​⌋)

考虑

f(n)=∑i=1n⌊ni⌋=∑k=1nd(k)\displaystyle f(n) = \sum_{i=1}^n\left\lfloor\frac{n}{i}\right\rfloor = \sum_{k=1}^nd(k) f(n)=i=1∑n​⌊in​⌋=k=1∑n​d(k)

则用线性筛求出 μ\muμ 和 ddd 的前缀和即可。
